# Quarterly reassessment and measurements

The program is **reassessed and restructured every 3 months (12 weeks)**. Between reassessments, follow-up happens in sessions and check-ins. Record everything in `clients/<name>/measurements.md` (template `templates/measurements-reassessment.md`).

## The training quarter

| Weeks | What |
|---|---|
| 0 | full reassessment and new program |
| 1 to 4 | block 1 (e.g., adaptation to the new program, main range) |
| 5 | deload week, if there are signs of fatigue or if scheduled |
| 6 to 11 | block 2 (progression; can change the range or add a technique) |
| 6 | mini-check: average weight, waist, optional photos |
| 12 | deload or testing week + full reassessment |

The base course recommends keeping the same stimulus for 6 to 12 weeks; the quarter fits that, with a variation midway if useful.

## How to measure at home (for comparable measurements)

**Conditions:** in the morning, fasted, after using the bathroom, before training, always the same way. Flexible tape measure, snug against the skin without squeezing, parallel to the floor. Measure each point twice and use the average. Ideally the same person measuring.

| Point | Where |
|---|---|
| Shoulders | widest loop, passing over the deltoids, arms relaxed |
| Chest | at nipple line, after a normal exhale |
| Arm (R and L) | midway between shoulder and elbow, arm relaxed at the side |
| Waist | at the narrowest circumference between the ribs and navel |
| Abdomen | at navel level |
| Hips | at the widest circumference of the glutes, feet together |
| Thigh (R and L) | just below the glute fold, or mid-thigh (choose one and keep it) |
| Calf (R and L) | at the widest circumference |

**Weight:** weigh 3 to 7 times per week under the same conditions and use the **weekly average**. A single weigh-in varies 1 to 2 kg from water, salt, digestion and menstrual cycle.

**Photos:** front, side and back; same light, clothes, distance, position and time.

**Bioimpedance (if available):** always on the same device, same conditions (fasted, no training before, usual hydration). Use it to see the trend, not a single number.

## Full reassessment outline

1. **Conversation:** what did they think of the quarter (0 to 10), what they liked, what they would change; is the goal still the same? Did anything change in routine, health, tastes or interests? (update the profile)
2. **PAR-Q** again, if health changed or it has been 12 months.
3. **Adherence:** workouts done vs planned in the quarter (from the session logs).
4. **Performance:** recent best set of the key lifts (load x reps, RIR) and comparison with the start. Estimated 1RM optional (e.g., load x (1 + reps/30)), only as a reference of progress.
5. **Measurements, average weight and photos** (protocol above).
6. **Simple tests:** max push-ups with good form (type of push-up), plank in seconds, bodyweight squat (quality), shoulder, hip and ankle mobility.
7. **Pain in the period:** what appeared, what resolved, what persists.
8. **Nutrition** (if working with a dietitian): quarter summary to bring to the dietitian.
9. **Conclusion and plan:** what improved with numbers, what did not move and why, measurable goals for the next quarter, and the **new program** (exercises, split, ranges, techniques).

## Interpreting measurements

| Weight | Waist | Arm/thigh/glute measures and loads | Likely reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| stable | decreasing | increasing | muscle gain with fat loss (recomposition) |
| slowly rising | stable | increasing | mass gain with little fat |
| rising fast | rising | stable | excess energy; talk to the dietitian |
| falling | falling | stable or loads rising | fat loss preserving muscle |
| falling | falling | falling, loads falling | large deficit or poor recovery; talk to the dietitian |

Always look at the trend of several measures together, never a single number. Celebrate progress with concrete numbers.
