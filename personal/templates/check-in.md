# Check-in (every 1 to 2 weeks)

Send to the client in short blocks. Then decide with `references/progression.md` and log it in the client's profile.

## Questions for the client

**Block 1, adherence**
1. How many workouts did you do since the last check-in, out of how many planned?
2. Did any workout end halfway? Why (time, tiredness, pain, crowded gym)?

**Block 2, performance**
3. Loads and reps on the main exercises (paste your notes).
4. In the last sets, how many reps could you still do with good form (RIR)?
5. Was any exercise too easy or too hard?

**Block 3, body and recovery**
6. Any pain? Where, 0 to 10, what does it feel like and when does it show up?
7. How is your sleep (hours and quality)? And your energy during the day?
8. Were you sore for more than 3 days after any workout?

**Block 4, mindset**
9. From 0 to 10, how motivated are you to train?
10. What did you like most and what would you change?

**Block 5, nutrition (only with a dietitian, if the client wants)**
11. Do you want the week's summary compared with your dietitian's plan?

(In week 6 of the quarter, also ask for average weight and waist. Full measurements, photos and the new program wait for the quarterly reassessment.)

## Quick reading of the answers

| Signal | Reading | Action (details in `progression.md`) |
|---|---|---|
| Hit the top of the range at the agreed RIR | ready to progress | raise the load |
| Inside the range, improving | on track | keep |
| Reps dropping over 2 sessions in a row, bad sleep, sore for many days | accumulated fatigue | deload week |
| 3 weeks or more with no load or rep progress | plateau | check sleep, nutrition and adherence; then change the stimulus |
| Joint pain, score 4 or more, or pain that worsens with training | warning | remove or swap the exercise; if it persists, refer |
| Trained less than half of what was planned | plan does not fit the routine | reduce days or duration before changing the content |
| Low motivation | dropout risk | talk, vary exercises or techniques, review the goal |

## Log entry in the profile
`| date | workouts done/planned | pain (0 to 10) | sleep | effort | decision made |`
