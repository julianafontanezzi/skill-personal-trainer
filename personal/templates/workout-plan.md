## How to execute and log
- **Rule 1:** in every set, go to the agreed **safe** maximum (RIR above). If you can pass the top of the range, keep going and note it.
- **Rule 2:** the result must fall inside the range. Past the top, raise the load on the next set or next session; below the bottom, lower it.
- It is normal for reps to drop a little in later sets.
- Log every session: load and reps for each set of the main exercises, and the RIR.
- Lowering slower than lifting, full range of motion without pain.

## Safety
- Stop immediately and get help if you feel chest pain, unusual shortness of breath, dizziness, palpitations or sharp joint pain. Emergency: call your local emergency number (e.g., 911, 112; Brazil: 192).
- Muscle soreness the next day is normal; joint pain, shooting pain or pain that gets worse during the exercise is not: stop the exercise and tell me.
